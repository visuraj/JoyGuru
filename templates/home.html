Param Premamay Sree Sree Thakur Anukulchandra

Do never die, nor cause death but resist death to death.

With this immortal call, Param Premamaya Sree Sree Thakur Anukulchandra, Fulfiller of the Best of the Age, awakened slumbering minds and rejuvenated innumerable dwindling souls. His boundless love embraced humanity, inspiring each individual towards the continuous journey of Being and Becoming.

His philosophy of spirituality encompasses every dimension of human existence — from science and education to economic growth, social transformation and political reformation.

His ideology touched the entirety of life, illuminating the path for humanity to continually evolve in thought, character and action.

Satsang — A Growing Movement

Satsang is a man-making organisation that grew around the life and teachings of Param Premamaya Sree Sree Thakur Anukulchandra. Inspired by His vision, it seeks to renew the world by nurturing the growth and transformation of each individual.

Today, under the aegis of Param Pujyapad Sree Sree Acharyadev, Satsang continues to bring meaningful change to the lives of millions, with a presence that spans the globe. People of every background, caste, creed, language, and nationality come together in shared devotion to the path, with the Living Ideal shining as their guiding star.

Satsang Vihar Bengaluru

In a city known for innovation, enterprise and a fast-paced life, Satsangees in Bengaluru came together with a shared purpose — to spread the love and message of Sree Sree Thakur, in keeping with the wish of Param Pujyapad Sree Sree Acharyadev.

This collective effort led to the establishment of Satsang Vihar at Medahalli, Bengaluru, inaugurated in 2014. Since then, it has been a centre for spiritual gatherings, cultural activities, community engagement and service, bringing the teachings of Sree Sree Thakur closer to people across the city.

To know more visit satsang.org.in
